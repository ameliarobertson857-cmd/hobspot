Electronic Window for Professionals

The Electronic Window for Professionals (VEC)  is an internet site from which SAS professionals or candidates for selection processes can carry out procedures electronically.

The Andalusian Regional Government's payment platform requires a digital certificate and the Autofirma application installed on your computer to make online payments. If you do not have these and/or do not wish to install them, please remember that for your registration in the competitive examination, in addition to online payment, you have the option of printing your payment slip and paying at a bank branch, and you can upload proof of payment to your VEC account.

It is a completely secure service since all information transmitted from the VEC is encrypted. Furthermore, thanks to authentication via Digital Certificate or cl@ve, it is guaranteed that only the intended user can access their information.

For assistance in using the application,  you can contact the VEC Support Service of the Andalusian Health Service, on weekdays from Monday to Friday from 08:00h to 15:00h at the telephone number 955 01 71 09.
